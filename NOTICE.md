## Required Planet adapted-material notices

Planet's [Tanager catalog root](https://www.planet.com/data/stac/tanager-core-imagery/catalog.json)
states that redistributions use its specified attribution and that adapted
material is prefaced with “Adapted from...”. For this project, use these exact,
year-specific notices (both apply):

```text
Adapted from Tanager STAC Data, available at www.planet.com/data/stac © 2024 Planet Labs PBC. All Rights Reserved.
Adapted from Tanager STAC Data, available at www.planet.com/data/stac © 2025 Planet Labs PBC. All Rights Reserved.
```
